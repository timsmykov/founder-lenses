# Two worked examples

These are invented examples to make the decision process inspectable, not measured client results.

## Duplicate weekly report

A team manually copies the same metrics from a dashboard into a weekly document. Nobody has shown that the document is read or needed for a decision. A scheduled agent has been proposed to create it.

**Decision:** do not automate yet. Identify the decision the document serves and its owner; compare the dashboard with the report. If the owner confirms no requirement, try skipping one issue with a clear way to restore it. If the report is required, simplify the input and then consider a scheduled draft with review.

**Unknowns:** readership, actual legal or management requirements, exceptions and the cost of omission. No time-saving estimate is implied.

## Regulated audit record

A team manually reviews a required audit record for completeness. The record is mandated, but the exact work instructions and exception rate have not been checked.

**Decision:** retain the required record and responsible reviewer. Inspect the actual obligation and representative cases. Test whether a tool can flag missing fields for a human to review; do not automate approval or remove the record on the basis of this example.

**Unknowns:** applicable rules, error rate, access constraints and whether the flagger improves the accepted outcome.
