# Evidence and attribution

This skill is a new editorial synthesis grounded in [Founder Systems](https://github.com/timsmykov/founder-systems). It draws on the reviewed comparison `R:f56ffb24725bb325` and decision `C:nik-02:nik-02:L017` in the [public comparison dataset](https://github.com/timsmykov/founder-systems/blob/main/data/relations.json).

The Nik-side material discusses applying systems or models to standardized work (`G:hiring-01:I085`). The book-side material discusses questioning and deleting unnecessary process before automation (`G:elon-02:I052`), attributed to Musk through Eric Jorgenson's edited *The Book of Elon*. The combined procedure is our own proposed application; neither source establishes that the two founders prescribed it together. Source-time conditions, edge cases and human professional oversight matter. The book's original material and recordings were not independently authenticated by this case.

The skill is a decision aid, not a validated method or a proxy for either person's current advice. It includes no complete source texts or copied private research artifacts.
